# Outreach templates

Copy-paste, then fill the `[brackets]`. Tone throughout: **a local who built a
free tool for the scene** — humble, specific, no startup-speak. Keep it short.

> Replace `bandconnect.app` with your real domain, and `@bandconnectct` with
> your real handle.

---

## 1. Venue — cold email (first touch)

**Subject:** Free listing for [Venue]'s shows on a new CT calendar

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [Your name], a [musician/fan] from [town]. I built **BandConnect**, a free
> calendar of every live show in Connecticut — fans filter by town, genre,
> family-friendly, and no-cover, then RSVP and get reminders.
>
> I've **already added [Venue]'s next few shows** so your page isn't blank:
> **[link to /p/venue-slug]**. Your events also generate Google "event" rich
> results and a one-tap add-to-calendar — better reach than an Instagram post,
> and it's $0.
>
> Want me to hand you the keys so you can post your own shows? Takes two minutes
> to claim it.
>
> Either way, thanks for everything you do for the scene.
> — [Your name] · [phone] · [link]

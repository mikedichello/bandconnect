# CT venues & supply — onboarding target list

Where the **supply** lives: venues that book live/original music, plus the
places gigging musicians congregate (open mics, record stores, studios, radio).
Work this list with the [outreach templates](outreach-templates.md) and log every
touch in the [tracker](outreach-tracker.csv). See the [plan](../GTM.md) for how
this fits the launch.

> ⚠️ **Verify before you send.** Contacts below were surfaced by web research in
> June 2026. Even those marked "(public)" must be **confirmed on the venue's
> official booking page** before outreach — an address may be outdated or
> mis-transcribed. Items marked `verify` are unconfirmed. **Never** mass-mail;
> this is opt-in B2B outreach about a *free* listing.
